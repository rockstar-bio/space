# Content policy

Space may host or link only to firmware, tools, documentation, and other resources that are legally redistributable. Contributors must have the right to share submitted material or identify a license or authorization that permits redistribution.

Do not submit leaked, confidential, access-controlled, personally identifying, malicious, or clearly infringing material. Trademarks and third-party software remain the property of their respective owners. The MIT License applies only to original project code, not hosted content.

## Takedowns

If you believe content should be removed, report it privately through the repository maintainers' GitHub contact options. Include the file name or URL, the reason for the request, the relevant rights or authorization information, and a way to follow up. Do not include sensitive personal information unless necessary.

Maintainers will review reports promptly where possible, may temporarily restrict access while reviewing, and will document the decision and any required correction. This process does not create or claim a particular legal status or response deadline.

# Sponsorship

Space is a completely nonprofit, open-source project. It provides free public access to legally redistributable mobile firmware, repair resources, tools, and documentation, without advertising, paid downloads, subscriptions, or commercial monetization.

The principal infrastructure need is durable object storage and efficient public delivery. The initial request is approximately 10 TB of storage, with projected future growth toward 25 TB and roughly 2–10 TB of monthly public downloads. Large files commonly range from 5–20 GB.

Cloudflare is a prospective sponsor for this work. Project Alexandria sponsorship has been requested, but no sponsorship, credits, or storage allocation has been granted. See [the application](docs/CLOUDFLARE_SPONSORSHIP.md) for context.

Sponsorship would help keep the service free, support a verified migration, and make the project sustainable for repair communities with limited budgets.

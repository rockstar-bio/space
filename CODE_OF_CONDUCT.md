# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include using welcoming language, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, public or private intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository maintainers' GitHub contact options. Reports will be reviewed and handled fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, restrict participation, or take other action they consider appropriate.
